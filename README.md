# Gaussian Elimination — The Canteen Puzzle

**M3 Linear Algebra and Calculus**  
**Student** Ankit Pandit · e25b070753

Three college canteen bills form a system of equations for the prices of chai, samosa, and Maggi. The animation follows the system through forward elimination, back-substitution, and verification.

- `Video.mp4` — final narrated 5:44 animation in 1920 × 1080.
- `Storyboard.pdf` — six scenes with timestamps, final video frames, narration cues and mathematics.
- `Script.md` — narration script and scene timing.
- `Code/` — editable HyperFrames source, assets, audio and render instructions.
